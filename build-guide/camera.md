# Prepare the controller and camera

{{BOM}}

## Camera {pagestep}

Connect the [OV5640 camera]{Qty: 1} to the camera connector of the
[XIAO ESP32S3 Sense]{Qty: 1}: lift the latch, slide the ribbon in fully, close the latch.
Remove the protective film from the lens.

## Antenna {pagestep}

Plug the [Wi-Fi antenna]{Qty: 1} into the XIAO's U.FL connector until it clicks. QC checks
it twice: by asking you, and by scanning for networks.

## SD card {pagestep}

Insert a [microSD card]{Qty: 1} (FAT32 or exFAT) into the slot until it clicks.
